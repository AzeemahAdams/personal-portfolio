Overview

What is EduTech?

EduTech is a fully responsive e-learning web application that lets users discover, enrol in, and track online courses across a wide range of subjects — from programming and design to business and personal development.

The interface is built around clarity and focus, minimising distractions so learners can stay in flow. The platform is deployed live on Vercel and accessible from any device.
