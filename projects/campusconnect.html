Overview

What is CampusConnect?

CampusConnect is a student-focused web platform created to make campus life more connected and engaging. The idea is to give students a central digital space where they can connect with others and easily interact with their campus community.

The project focuses on creating a simple, accessible and responsive experience while exploring important frontend concepts such as user authentication, form handling, JavaScript and browser-based data storage.

Student Connection

Designed to help students connect and interact with other members of their campus community.

Account Creation

Students can create an account through a simple and user-friendly registration experience.

Login System

A frontend login flow allows registered users to access their CampusConnect experience.

Local Storage

Browser localStorage is used to store user information and demonstrate client-side data persistence.

Simple User Experience

The interface is designed to be straightforward and easy for students to navigate.

Responsive Design

Built to provide a consistent experience across mobile, tablet and desktop screens.
